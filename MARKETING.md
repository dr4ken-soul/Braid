# Braid Marketing and Ecosystem Plan

Status: optional
Submission requirement: not required

## 1. Purpose

The challenge submission is the Idea Canvas. A post on X is not a substitute for the canvas and should not delay the build.

Use marketing only after the hosted demo works.

## 2. Positioning

Braid turns a multilingual insurance claim conversation into a source-linked evidence packet for a human adjuster.

Short description:

Braid listens to a claimant, compares the account with policy and repair records, exposes contradictions, and routes unresolved decisions to an adjuster.

Avoid claims that Braid approves, denies, settles, or detects fraud.

## 3. Optional single X post

Keep the final post specific to Braid. Do not use invented customer names, production claims, or real claimant data.

Braid turns an English or Arabic claim call into an evidence packet.
